3D Deburr works in the CAM workspace. Switch to CAM and open a setup to use it.

Breaks sharp outside edges reachable from above. Wavy or sloped edges are cut at one depth for now, so their break is uneven (waiting on PhysCAD). Check in simulation before cutting.

Which edges: every sharp edge, unless you pick some.

To deburr only some edges, pick them on the part. With none picked, every sharp edge is deburred.
